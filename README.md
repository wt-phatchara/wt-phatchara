# Phatchara Wipataphan

**Founder, In'life · AI-IoT engineer · battery systems researcher** · Rayong, Thailand

I build systems that live quietly inside real life: battery storage, edge devices and AI agents, designed, built and running in Thailand.

## What I build

- **Battery packs that survive the tropics**: UPS and solar storage with LFP, NMC90 and supercapacitors; packs tested side by side with every cycle logged.
  - LFP 8S2P vs NMC90 7S12P matched at 30 Ah
  - UPS telemetry station with off-site archive and alarm layer
- **Firmware that reports from the field**: ESP32 firmware reading BMS over UART and BLE, telemetry over WiFi, surviving reboots and missing clocks.
  - Solar remote light fleet firmware + host tools
  - Scooter BMS bus reverse-engineered listen-only with an ESP32
- **An AI team that runs around the clock**: 10 AI agents run day and night on a 5-machine mesh; each task goes to the cheapest model that is safe for its data; secrets stay local.
  - 5 machines on a private mesh
  - Plain-language control from Discord with an approval gate and audit log
- **Websites people use in a room**: Interactive 3D and motion websites built and shipped on Cloudflare.
  - Classroom 3D game site 'New Planet, New Home', live on 2 Pages sites
  - web-studio deck-to-3D-website kit

Most of this runs in private repositories (client and research work). Happy to walk through any of it.
